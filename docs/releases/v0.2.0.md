# Glowcast v0.2.0

See and control Glowcast from Home Assistant. Still an early version.

## What changed

- **The settings are entities on Glowcast's device, changeable while syncing.** Brightness and Intensity (sliders), Update rate and Entertainment area are under **Configuration** on the device page. Change them there, from a dashboard, by voice or with an automation (for example softer lights late in the evening). Brightness, intensity and the update rate now change while syncing without restarting the light stream; only another entertainment area restarts it, and the switch stays on (#13).

  <img width="400" alt="Glowcast's device page in Home Assistant while syncing: the switch on under Controls, the status Streaming under Sensors, and the settings under Configuration." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/device-page.png">

  *Glowcast's device page: the switch under Controls, the status under Sensors, the settings under Configuration.*
- **See what Glowcast does.** A **Status** sensor shows Idle, Starting, Streaming, Black picture, No picture, TV off or Error, with a **Stop reason** attribute (for example `tv_off` or `output_busy`) to use in automations. A **Capture rate** sensor shows how many pictures per second Glowcast gets from the TV; it is a diagnostic sensor and off by default (#14). The [README](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast#seeing-what-glowcast-does) explains each status.
- **The settings screen says where else the settings are.** Under **Configure**, a note says the same settings are on the device page (#144).

  <img width="400" alt="Glowcast's settings screen in Home Assistant: a note that the same settings are on the device page under Configuration, then entertainment area, brightness 80 %, intensity 100 %, update rate High (3 per second)." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/settings.png">
- **No picture, no light show.** When the TV gives no picture at all, for example a streaming app with DRM in the TV's own apps, Glowcast first keeps the last colours for a moment, then the faint glow, and after 7 seconds of an all-zero picture it lets go of the lights, so they return to how they were before syncing. The switch stays on, and Glowcast follows again as soon as there is a picture (#68, #139). A dark scene in a film is not all zero, so it keeps the glow as before.
- **A clearer stop when the TV cannot be captured.** Glowcast stops after 5 failed captures in a row and at least 3 seconds, so a short hiccup no longer stops it at the higher update rates (#110).
- **Diagnostics for bug reports.** **Settings → Devices & services → Glowcast → ⋮ → Download diagnostics** gives the versions, your settings, the TV's and bridge's model and software, the measured captures per second and what Glowcast was doing last, also after it stopped. Keys, addresses, ids, serial numbers and names are left out (#21).
- **A new logo and illustrations.** Glowcast has a new icon (a glowing strip), and the README shows what it does with illustrations and screenshots of the setup flow, the settings screen and the device page (#135, #87).

  <img width="400" alt="An illustrated living room: the picture on the TV changes colour and the light strip and lamps follow it." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/illustrations/scene-1-picture-leads-loop.webp">
- **Setup asks what the lights follow.** The setup starts with **What the lights follow**: **LG webOS TV** or **Computer screen**. For a TV, the steps are the same as before (#66).

  <img width="400" alt="The Glowcast setup flow in Home Assistant: choose the TV, choose the Hue bridge, press the link button and wait for the pairing, choose the entertainment area, done." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/setup-flow.gif">

  *Setting up Glowcast for a TV: the Hue bridge and its link button, then the entertainment area.*
- **Preview for developers: a computer screen as the picture.** Glowcast can follow the screen of a PC or Mac through a small screen helper on that computer. Home Assistant discovers the helper on your network, and you pair it with a 6-digit code. For now the helper is a developer tool that you start from a clone of this repository, and it has not been checked on a real PC with real lights yet; see [the screen helper](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast/blob/main/screen_helper/README.md) (#108, #110, #109).
- Inside: a local Home Assistant demo that records the flows, a tool that shows what Glowcast sends to the lights before and after a change, a test bench with a real Hue light and a webcam (#134), and an end-to-end test in CI with the screen helper's test patterns (#112).

Checked on a 2023 LG G3 with a Hue Bridge Pro and a gradient lightstrip: following the picture, pure colours, black pictures and the TV off, a protected app (Prime Video), switching HDMI inputs, the settings while syncing, a removed entertainment area, the diagnostics and the blueprint (#129).

## Known limits

- **What the lights do after a stop** follows the entertainment area's "When syncing stops" setting in the Hue app: with "Last on-state" the bridge restores them; with "Pause on the last colour" they keep Glowcast's last colour, while Home Assistant still shows their earlier state (#142).
- **Protected apps in the TV's own apps** release the lights after about 7 seconds of an all-zero picture. An app's start screen (a logo on black) is not all zero, so it may delay the release: with Prime Video, the lights were released about 14 seconds after the film started.
- The other limits of v0.1 still apply, see [Known limits](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast#known-limits) in the README.

## Upgrading from v0.1.1

Update Glowcast in HACS (or under Settings → System → Updates) and restart Home Assistant. Your settings stay as they are, and the switch keeps its entity id. The new entities appear on Glowcast's device by themselves; the Capture rate sensor is off until you turn it on.

## Requirements and installation

The same as v0.1.0: Home Assistant 2025.7.2 or newer, an LG webOS TV and a Hue Bridge v2 or Bridge Pro with an entertainment area, both set up in Home Assistant. See the [README](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast#installation) for the installation steps.
