# Glowcast v0.3.0

More lights, and ready to share: any colour light in Home Assistant can follow the picture now, also together with a Hue entertainment area, and Glowcast has a website with a user guide. Still an early version.

## What changed

- **Loose lights: any colour light follows the picture, each at its own place.** Besides a Hue entertainment area, Glowcast can now drive colour lights in Home Assistant of any brand: Hue lights, Zigbee bulbs and strips, WLED, Matter lights and others. No Hue bridge is needed. Pick single lights, a Home Assistant area or a light group, and give each light its place: left, right, above or below the screen, ambient (the middle of the picture), or fine-tuned with two sliders. Loose lights change about once per second, with a fade, and go back to how they were when Glowcast stops. On the device page, a **Lights** select chooses the area or light group that follows the picture (#9). See [Loose lights](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/loose-lights/).

- **An entertainment area and loose lights together.** One Glowcast can drive both, with one switch: for example the Hue lights round the TV in an entertainment area, and a non-Hue strip or a Matter lamp beside the sofa as loose lights. Choose **Entertainment area and loose lights** under **Configure**. The area keeps its own pace (up to 25 changes per second) and the loose lights theirs; the loose lights never slow the area down. If one of the two cannot start or is lost while syncing, the other keeps following the picture, Glowcast tries the lost one again, and the Status sensor's **Dropped lights** attribute (`dropped_outputs`) says which part is not running (#11). See [Together with an entertainment area](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/loose-lights/#together-with-an-entertainment-area).

  *Adding loose lights to an entertainment area under Configure.*
- **A calm start: the lights fade in from how they were.** When Glowcast takes the lights over, they fade in slowly, in about 2 seconds, from their own state to the picture's colours: a lamp in warm white goes from warm white to the picture, a light that was off fades up from dark. The same happens when the picture comes back after a moment without one. With an entertainment area, a light that was bright may dip a little at the very start before it fades to the picture, because the stream cannot show a light's own full white (#153).

  <img width="400" alt="The illustrated living room: the TV turns on with a sunset, and the lights that were off fade up from dark to the picture's colours in about 2 seconds." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/fade-in.gif">

  *Lights that were off fade up to the picture's colours.*
- **Texts that fit a computer screen too.** The status **TV off** is now **Picture source off**, with the stop reason "Picture source off or not reachable", and the log lines name the picture source instead of the TV. The keys for automations (`tv_off`, `source_unavailable` and the others) stay the same (#155).
- **The bridge's model in the diagnostics.** On Home Assistant 2026.10, the diagnostics show the Hue bridge's manufacturer, model and software version again, still without its name, address or serial number (#163).
- **A website with the documentation and a user guide.** [nicohabets.github.io/glowcast](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/) has a landing page, the full documentation and a [user guide](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/guide/) that takes you from installing to the first evening (#167, #20). The README is now the short version. The **Documentation** link on Glowcast's page in Home Assistant opens the website's documentation (#177).

  <img width="400" alt="The top of Glowcast's website on a phone: Your Hue lights follow the picture on your LG TV, the illustrated living room and the button Install with HACS." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/website.png">
- **The website counts its visits.** It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies and without storing anything in your browser; each page loads one script from Cloudflare that reports the page, the site you came from, your browser, your country and how fast the page loaded. This is only about the website: Glowcast itself, in Home Assistant, never sends anything outside your home (#190). See [Privacy](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/privacy/#this-website).

Checked on the test bench with real lights: loose lights, an entertainment area and loose lights together, and the fade-in at every start (#9, #11, #153).

## For contributors

Nothing changes for you as a user.

- **Dependabot** proposes updates for the GitHub Actions, the screen helper and the website (#191).
- **A release zip.** Publishing a release attaches `glowcast.zip`, which HACS installs from; its download count is a rough count of installs and updates (#189, see [Releasing](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast/blob/main/docs/releasing.md)).
- **The room with loose lights.** `python3 scripts/demo.py room --lights loose|combined` shows loose lights at their places, and an entertainment area and loose lights together, through Glowcast's real loose-lights output (#196).
- **The test bench** shows a Glowcast rest screen and vivid two-colour scenes in screen mode, so every light changes colour at every scene (#168), has suites for loose lights and for an entertainment area with loose lights (#9, #11), and no longer locates a light on its own clipped lens (#174).

## Upgrading from v0.2.0

Update Glowcast in HACS (or under Settings → System → Updates) and restart Home Assistant. Your settings stay as they are, and the entities keep their entity ids. Your entry keeps its entertainment area; loose lights are an extra choice under **Configure**.

- **The status texts** say **Picture source off** instead of **TV off**. The state and the stop reason keys are unchanged, so automations keep working.
- **New on the device page:** with loose lights, the **Lights** select takes the place of **Entertainment area**; with both together, the device page has both. With both together, the Status sensor has the **Dropped lights** attribute (`dropped_outputs`) while one part is not running.
- **The fade-in** at the start has no setting; it is always on.

## Installation through HACS

v0.3.0 is the first release that HACS installs from the release file `glowcast.zip` instead of from the source code (#189). Nothing changes in the steps: open Glowcast in HACS and update or download it as before. GitHub counts how often that file is downloaded; that is the only count, and nothing is sent from your Home Assistant.

## Known limits

- **Loose lights change about once per second**, and at most 4 commands per second go to each Hue bridge and each Thread network, so with more lights each changes less often (#125). A gradient lightstrip gets one colour as a loose light.
- **A loose light that is also in the entertainment area.** The settings refuse a light in both, but a loose light that is later added to the entertainment area in the Hue app is driven twice, by the stream and by light commands. Pick the loose lights again under Configure; a fix is planned for v0.3.1 (#198).
- **Changing the loose lights of an entertainment area and loose lights together** needs the Hue bridge to be reachable, because the settings start with the area; a fix is planned for v0.3.1 (#197).
- **What the lights of an entertainment area do after a stop** follows the area's "When syncing stops" setting in the Hue app: with "Last on-state" the bridge restores them; with "Pause on the last colour" they keep Glowcast's last colour, while Home Assistant still shows their earlier state (#142). Loose lights always go back to how they were.
- **Protected apps in the TV's own apps** release the lights after about 7 seconds of an all-zero picture. An app's start screen (a logo on black) is not all zero, so it may delay the release.
- The other limits still apply (about 3 captures per second, only LG webOS TVs, one stream per bridge), see [Known limits](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/limits/) on the website.

## Requirements and installation

Home Assistant 2025.7.2 or newer and an LG webOS TV set up in Home Assistant, as before. For the lights, one of: a Hue Bridge v2 or Bridge Pro with an entertainment area, set up in Home Assistant (the smoothest), or colour lights in Home Assistant as loose lights, of any brand, without a Hue bridge. See [Installation](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/installation/) on the website.
