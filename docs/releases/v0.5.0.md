# Glowcast v0.5.0

Works on your TV, and starts right: ready for TVs with webOS 26 and a first step for older webOS TVs, a clear message when a TV refuses screenshots, a button to check where each light sits, a hardware check for TVs we have not tested, and a blueprint that starts only after dark or for the inputs you choose. Still an early version.

**Home Assistant 2026.8.3 or newer is now required** (see [Upgrading from v0.4.0](#upgrading-from-v040)).

The images below come from a local demo with a fake TV and fake lights, in Home Assistant's phone view.

## What changed

- **Ready for TVs with webOS 26.** LG's 2026 update, webOS 26, only pairs with Home Assistant 2026.8.3 or newer, so Glowcast now needs that version too (#213). It is the minimum that can work on webOS 26, not a guarantee: Glowcast has not been tried on such a TV yet (see [Known limits](#known-limits)).
- **A first step for older webOS TVs.** Glowcast now asks the TV for a picture of 960x540 pixels, the size it already got from the tested TV. On a 2019 LG TV with webOS 4, the TV's capture service was reported to refuse a full-screen picture without a size and to work with this one; whether that makes older TVs work with Glowcast is not checked yet (#221).
- **A TV that refuses screenshots says so.** Glowcast sees the picture through the TV's screenshot call, which LG does not document. A TV that does not offer or does not permit that call used to look like a TV that was off. Now Glowcast stops with the status **Error** and the stop reason `source_not_supported` or `source_not_permitted`, and Home Assistant shows a repair that says what happened and what may help (#214, #253). See [The TV does not allow screenshots](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/troubleshooting/#the-tv-does-not-allow-screenshots).

- **Test lights.** A new button on Glowcast's device page, under **Configuration**, shows red on the left and blue on the right, then green at the top and white below, about 3 seconds each, so you can see where each light sits without waiting for the right scene (#182, #270). It works while Glowcast syncs and while it is off (the TV does not need to be on), with an entertainment area, Home Assistant lights or both. See [Test the lights](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/settings/#test-lights).

  *The device page: Test lights under Configuration, Run hardware check under Diagnostic.*

- **The hardware check.** To make Glowcast work on TVs we have not tested, we need facts you cannot read off a settings screen. The new **Run hardware check** button, under **Diagnostic**, collects them in up to about 30 seconds: the TV's model and software, whether it answers the screenshot call and how fast, and what the Hue bridge and the entertainment area offer. It only reads; it starts no stream and changes no light. A notification says when it is done, and the result is in the diagnostics download, without names, addresses, keys or pictures. Please attach it to a [hardware report](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast/issues/new?template=hardware-report.md), also when Glowcast just works (#115, #270). The button is there when Glowcast uses an LG webOS TV or a Hue entertainment area. See [The hardware check](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/troubleshooting/#the-hardware-check).

  *Run hardware check, and the notification when it is done.*

- **Start only when it fits.** The blueprint "Start Glowcast when the TV turns on" has two new optional settings: **Only after dark** (while the sun is below the horizon) and **Only for these inputs or apps** (for example "HDMI 2" or "Netflix"; Glowcast also starts when you switch to one of them while the TV is on). With **Also stop when the TV turns off** on, switching to an input or app that is not in the list turns Glowcast off again, but only when the blueprint started it (#161). See [The blueprint](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/starting/#the-blueprint).

- **Next to a Hue Sync Box.** A new blueprint, "Follow a Hue Sync Box", turns Glowcast on when a Philips Hue Play HDMI Sync Box starts syncing and off when it stops, so Home Assistant lights outside the box's area join in. It works with the box's "Light sync" switch (from the Hue Sync Box integration) or with the Hue integration's binary sensor of the box's entertainment area. When one of Glowcast's Home Assistant lights is a Hue light in an area that another app syncs, Home Assistant shows a repair that names it (#219). See [Next to a Hue Sync Box](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/guide/lights/#next-to-a-hue-sync-box).
- **Settings that fit on a phone.** The first step of the setup's lights and of **Configure** now fits on a phone screen: the question reads **Which lights follow the picture?**, the choices **Hue entertainment area - smoothest**, **Home Assistant lights - any brand** and, under Configure, **Hue area and Home Assistant lights** (#238, #277). In Dutch, **Hue-entertainmentruimte - vloeiendst** and **Losse lampen - elk merk**. Only the labels changed; your settings stay as they are.

- **The website** says "Your lights follow the picture on your LG TV" and names both kinds of lights (#209), explains what happens with a Home Assistant light that was added to the entertainment area later and with the settings while the bridge cannot be reached (#205), and its device page images show the two new buttons (#275).

## For contributors

Nothing changes for you as a user.

- **The minimum Home Assistant** in CI is 2026.8.3 (Python 3.14, `aiowebostv` 0.9.2); the compatibility code for older versions is gone (#213).
- **A source can refuse**: the port has `SourceNotSupported`, the pipeline stops with its own stop reason at the first one while syncing (at the start when the startup grace ends on one), and a source type brings its own repair issues (`stop_issues`), as an output type does (#214, #253).
- **Probes**: each source and output type can bring a read-only `probe` for the hardware check; the generic code runs them side by side with time limits and knows no type (#115, #270). An output type can have several notices (#219).
- **The light test** (`core/light_test.py`, no Home Assistant imports) runs fixed pattern frames through the colour engine, in the running pipeline or on its own while off (#182, #270).
- **Starts, stops, the light test and the hardware check** no longer overlap: the runtime ends one before the next starts, also at unload and when Home Assistant stops (#269, #282).
- **The screen helper's** pattern cycle no longer shows a pattern from before its start (#216). **The test bench** judges a Home Assistant light from Glowcast's first command in a step (#217) and presses Test lights in the basic suite (#182).
- **The demo** has flows for the blueprint, Test lights, the hardware check and a refusing TV (`GLOWCAST_DEMO_REFUSE`); see `scripts/README.md`.
- **Three architect reviews** checked the v0.5 changes; their findings were fixed (#253, #269, #270, #282) or are in the Backlog with options (#271, #272). See the reviews on #230.

## Upgrading from v0.4.0

**First check your Home Assistant version** (Settings → About). Glowcast 0.5.0 needs **Home Assistant 2026.8.3 or newer**. On an older Home Assistant, HACS will not install v0.5.0 (it says the version requires Home Assistant 2026.8.3 or newer), and the Glowcast you have keeps working as it is. Update Home Assistant first, then Glowcast. See [Home Assistant older than 2026.8.3?](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/installation/#home-assistant-older-than-202683).

Then update Glowcast in HACS (or under Settings → System → Updates) and restart Home Assistant. Your settings stay as they are, and the entities keep their entity ids.

- **Two new buttons** appear on Glowcast's device page: **Test lights** under Configuration and **Run hardware check** under Diagnostic (the latter with an LG webOS TV or a Hue entertainment area).
- **Renamed labels** in the setup and under Configure: the question and the choices of the lights step are shorter (see above). Nothing to do.
- **The blueprint's new settings** come with the new version of the blueprint. Home Assistant keeps its own copy of an imported blueprint, so to get **Only after dark** and **Only for these inputs or apps**, import it again: Settings → Automations & scenes → Blueprints, the menu (⋮) of "Start Glowcast when the TV turns on" → **Re-import blueprint**. Your automations keep their settings.

## Known limits

- **webOS 26:** Home Assistant 2026.8.3 is the minimum that can work, not a guarantee; a few TVs are reported to still refuse to pair. Glowcast has not been tried on a TV with webOS 26 yet.
- **The screenshot call is untested on webOS 26 and on older webOS TVs.** The picture size (#221) and the refusal message (#214) are built from reports, not checked on such a TV. Tried it? Please run the hardware check and [open a hardware report](https://github.com/nicohabets/glowcast/issues/new?template=hardware-report.md).
- **Next to a Hue Sync Box**, the notice for a light in an area another app syncs is checked only when Glowcast starts, and how it times with a real box is not checked yet (#272). Glowcast drives Home Assistant lights only there, not the box's own lights, and cannot use the box's colours.
- **HDR and Dolby Vision** have not been checked yet (#160).
- The other limits still apply (about 3 captures per second; LG webOS TVs only for now, tested on one model, with a computer screen as a preview; one stream per bridge), see [Known limits](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/limits/) on the website.

## Requirements and installation

Home Assistant 2026.8.3 or newer and an LG webOS TV set up in Home Assistant. For the lights, a Hue Bridge v2 or Bridge Pro with an entertainment area, colour lights in Home Assistant (Home Assistant lights), or both together. See [Installation](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/installation/) on the website.
