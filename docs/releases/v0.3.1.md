# Glowcast v0.3.1

Fixes after v0.3: a light in both the entertainment area and the loose lights is driven only once, the loose lights can be changed while the Hue bridge is down, and syncing continues after Home Assistant restarts. Still an early version.

## What changed

- **Syncing continues after a Home Assistant restart.** When Home Assistant restarts while Glowcast syncs (after an update, for example), Glowcast switches on again by itself once Home Assistant is back, if the TV (or the computer screen) still shows a picture (#183).
  - Glowcast looks for the picture every 10 seconds for 3 minutes after Home Assistant has started.
  - A TV that was turned off meanwhile, or a locked screen, is left alone: the switch stays off, and the automation starts Glowcast when the TV turns on, as before.
  - Glowcast stays off when it was off before the restart, when you switched it off, or when it had stopped by itself. A crash or power cut does not count.
  - Switching it on or off yourself while it looks ends the looking.
- **A loose light that was later added to the entertainment area.** With an entertainment area and loose lights together, a loose light that you add to the entertainment area in the Hue app is now left out of the loose lights, so it is no longer driven twice, by the stream and by light commands (#198).
  - Glowcast checks this every time it takes over the lights, and logs which lights it left out.
  - A repair, **Loose lights in the entertainment area**, names them and says how to fix it: choose the loose lights again without them under **Configure**, or remove them from the area in the Hue app. Glowcast checks it again each time it takes over the lights, and the repair goes away once the overlap is gone.
  - When all the loose lights are in the area, only the area syncs.
  - If the bridge cannot be asked, nothing is left out, as in the settings.
- **Change the loose lights while the Hue bridge cannot be reached.** Under **Configure** → **Entertainment area and loose lights**, with an entertainment area chosen before, the settings no longer stop when the bridge is down, has no entertainment areas, or no longer accepts Glowcast's key. A notice says the entertainment area cannot be changed right now. The area stays as it is, and you go on to the loose lights (#197). With a rejected key, Home Assistant still asks you to pair again.

## For contributors

Nothing changes for you as a user.

- **The output types share their helpers** (#199): the bridge's renderer → device → light join, the labels for names used twice, a light's colour at its brightness, and the "one type means no menu" choice in the settings. The tests are unchanged and the room before and after is identical; the only difference is with malformed bridge data (see #215).
- **The screen helper's cycle tests** run on a fake clock and no longer depend on how fast CI's runners are (#195).
- **The test bench** has a restart suite (`--suite restart`, #183) and an in-area step in the combined suite (`in_area: true`, #198).
- **Research:** which other TVs and devices speak the same webOS protocol and support the capture call (#185), and whether a Hue Sync Box exposes its colours and how Glowcast can drive other lights next to it (#202).

## Upgrading from v0.3.0

Update Glowcast in HACS (or under Settings → System → Updates) and restart Home Assistant. Your settings stay as they are, and the entities keep their entity ids.

- The restart that installs v0.3.1 still leaves the switch off, because v0.3.0 did not store whether it synced. From then on, Glowcast continues after a restart.

## Known limits

- **Loose lights change about once per second**, and at most 4 commands per second go to each Hue bridge and each Thread network, so with more lights each changes less often (#125). A gradient lightstrip gets one colour as a loose light.
- **What the lights of an entertainment area do after a stop** follows the area's "When syncing stops" setting in the Hue app: with "Last on-state" the bridge restores them; with "Pause on the last colour" they keep Glowcast's last colour, while Home Assistant still shows their earlier state (#142). Loose lights always go back to how they were.
- **Protected apps in the TV's own apps** release the lights after about 7 seconds of an all-zero picture. An app's start screen (a logo on black) is not all zero, so it may delay the release.
- The other limits still apply (about 3 captures per second, only LG webOS TVs, one stream per bridge), see [Known limits](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/limits/) on the website.

## Requirements and installation

As for v0.3.0: Home Assistant 2025.7.2 or newer and an LG webOS TV set up in Home Assistant. For the lights, a Hue Bridge v2 or Bridge Pro with an entertainment area, colour lights in Home Assistant as loose lights, or both together. See [Installation](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/installation/) on the website.
