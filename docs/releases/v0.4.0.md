# Glowcast v0.4.0

Colours that match the picture: livelier colours instead of a grey average, no grey lights from black bars, a lightstrip that follows the picture's edges, calmer lights at the back of the room and scene cuts that show at once. Still an early version.

The rooms below compare v0.3.1 (left) with v0.4 (right), with the same pictures, through Glowcast's own colour code. They are drawings from the room tool, not photos.

## What changed

- **Livelier colours.** Each light used to get the plain average of its part of the picture. When that part mixed a colourful object with a dull or dark background (spotlights on a stage, signs in a city at night), the average was a dull, dark colour that a higher intensity could not bring back. Now the colourful, bright pixels of each part count most. Such scenes show their colours. A white or evenly coloured part keeps its own colour, and a small coloured object only tints a white scene (#233, after spike #40). No new setting: intensity works as before, on top.

- **No grey lights from black bars.** In films with black bars above and below the picture (or beside it), the lights at the top and bottom used to sample the bar plus a thin strip of picture, and turned grey or dim when that strip was dark. Glowcast now finds the bars and takes every light's part of the picture from inside them (#34).
  - It waits about 2 seconds before it follows a new bar width, so a short dark scene or a fade to black does not move anything.
  - Subtitles in the bottom bar count as bar.

In v0.4 the glow round the TV shows the sky's light blue more clearly." src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicohabets/glowcast/main/docs/images/room-v040-letterbox.png">

  *A film with black bars: the strip's top takes the picture inside the bars.*

- **The lightstrip follows the edges of the picture.** The segments of a gradient lightstrip round the TV sit close to the centre in the Hue app, so they used to show the colours about a third in from the edges. Now the strip is stretched to the screen: its left and right segments show the picture's left and right edges, and its top segments the top edge, as if the picture spills over the edge of the screen (#23). Other lamps, and gradient lights elsewhere in the room, keep their place.

In v0.3.1 the glow round the TV is a pale pink, the colour of the middle of the picture; in v0.4 it is warmer at the left and violet-blue at the right.   *A sunset: the strip takes the picture's edges, and the lamp at the back of the room a soft mix of the picture.*

- **Lights at the back of the room follow more softly.** In an entertainment area, a lamp you put towards the back of the room in the Hue app (further from the TV) now blends its own colour with the colour of the whole picture: not at all near the TV, up to half at the back wall. The room feels calmer and the effect stays focused round the screen. In a dark scene the lamps at the back stay dim and never outshine the lights at the TV (#15, #262). Home Assistant lights have no front-to-back place, so they work as before. The sunset above shows it: the table lamp behind the sofa takes a soft pink from the whole picture.

- **Scene cuts show at once, small changes stay soft.** The lights fade from one picture to the next over the time between two captures. After a scene cut that made them about 0.2 seconds late, so they lagged behind the cut. Now a light whose colour jumps by a lot fades in 80 milliseconds, while small changes and slow drifts fade as softly as before (#258, after spike #35). On the test bench the delay from a change on the screen to the light went from about 0.42-0.48 to about 0.29-0.35 seconds (median). The fade to the glow after a dark scene stays soft. Home Assistant lights still change about once per second.

  *A cut to a red picture: v0.4 (right) reaches the new colour sooner.*

- **Home Assistant lights (formerly loose lights).** In English, "loose lights" is now called **Home Assistant lights**: any colour light, a Home Assistant area or a light group, of any brand (#207, #228). The settings read **Hue entertainment area**, **Home Assistant lights** and **Entertainment area and Home Assistant lights**, the last with a short line under it. The website's page moved to [Home Assistant lights](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/home-assistant-lights/); the old address forwards to it. In Dutch they stay **Losse lampen**. Nothing else changes: your settings, entities and automations keep working.
- **Screenshots of Home Assistant lights in the documentation.** The website shows the setup with Home Assistant lights, the place of each light, the settings screen's first step and the device page (#220).

  *Setting up Glowcast with Home Assistant lights.*

Checked on the test bench with real lights, each change against the version before it on the same evening:
- the colours, the hold on black, the glow, the release and the restore all pass;
- the delay from the screen to the light went down.

The bench has two single lamps near its screen, so the strip and the lamps at the back of the room were checked in the room tool and in tests.

## For contributors

Nothing changes for you as a user.

- **The colour engine** maps the lights onto the picture in one step over all channels, with the picture inside the letterbox bars (`core/letterbox.py`, pure, with the time as an argument). `Channel` has a depth `y` and the `light` it is a part of, which the Hue output fills in (#34, #23, #239).
- **Two architect reviews** checked the run's changes; their findings were fixed (#239, #240, #262, #263, #264) or are in the Backlog with options (#241, #265). See the reviews on #73.
- **The room** has a concert scenario, a lightstrip at a real strip's positions, and a lamp at the back of the room (#233, #23, #15). Its labels say Home Assistant lights; `room --lights loose` is now `--lights ha`, and the demo flows are `ha-lights-setup` and `ha-lights-settings` (#228).
- **The test bench:** `--suite loose` is now `--suite ha-lights`, its config key `ha_lights:` (the old name is still read), and its expected colours include the depth (#228, #263). Flaky camera checks in the evening are #231.
- **Tests for how the v0.4 changes work together** (`tests/test_v04_together.py`, #263).

## Upgrading from v0.3.1

Update Glowcast in HACS (or under Settings → System → Updates) and restart Home Assistant. Your settings stay as they are, and the entities keep their entity ids. Nothing to set: the new colours, the bars, the strip, the back lamps and the faster cuts work by themselves.

- **Lamps at the back of an entertainment area** now look calmer than before. If a lamp should follow its own part of the picture fully, move it towards the TV in the Hue app (Settings → Entertainment areas).
- **Intensity:** the colours are livelier at the same intensity. If you had raised it to make up for pale colours, you may want to lower it again.

## Known limits

- **A long dark band near an edge** (a night sky, a dark menu bar on a computer screen) counts as a black bar after about 2 seconds, so the lights take their colours a little further into the picture until the band goes (#241).
- **Sparkly content** (sparks, a starfield on black) can make a light flash up quickly and fade down slowly (#265).
- **HDR and Dolby Vision** have not been checked yet (#160).
- **Home Assistant lights change about once per second**, and at most 4 commands per second go to each Hue bridge and each Thread network (#125). A gradient lightstrip gets one colour as a Home Assistant light.
- **What the lights of an entertainment area do after a stop** follows the area's "When syncing stops" setting in the Hue app (#142). Home Assistant lights always go back to how they were.
- The other limits still apply (about 3 captures per second, only LG webOS TVs, one stream per bridge), see [Known limits](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/limits/) on the website.

## Requirements and installation

As for v0.3.1: Home Assistant 2025.7.2 or newer and an LG webOS TV set up in Home Assistant. For the lights, a Hue Bridge v2 or Bridge Pro with an entertainment area, colour lights in Home Assistant (Home Assistant lights), or both together. See [Installation](https://nicohabets.github.io/glowcast/docs/installation/) on the website.
